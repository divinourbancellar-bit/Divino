Wine Tasting at Divino Urban Cellar

22 June 2026 Every day, 6:00 PM 2 min read

An amazing opportunity to discover some of the finest wines Sicily has to offer — every day at 6:00 PM.

The tasting runs daily in the cellar at Via Gibilmanna 16. It takes about an hour, it is guided, and it is built around the same principle as everything else we do here: taste the wines next to each other and the island starts to make sense.

Three good reasons to join

A private tasting session. The session is personalised and intimate rather than a queue through a counter — you get to ask the awkward questions.

No need to drive. We are in the heart of Cefalù, within easy walking distance — which rather changes what you are willing to try.

The best wines of Sicily. Authentic Sicilian wineries, unique terroirs, and flavours that stay with you.

Taste Sicily, one glass at a time.

Book your wine tasting experience with us — reservations are recommended.
