Vaccaro: A Journey Into the Flavours of Sicily

30 August 2026 Event: 8 May, 18:30 1 min read

A journey into the flavours of Sicily, with the wines of the Vaccaro winery.

These producer evenings are the most useful thing we do. Tasting three or four wines from one estate in a row tells you something a single bottle never will: what the house actually believes about wine, and how that belief shows up in the glass.

Un viaggio nei sapori della Sicilia.

Enoteca Divino, 8 May 2026, 18:30. Booking recommended.
