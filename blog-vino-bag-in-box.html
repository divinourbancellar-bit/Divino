The Wine at Home Is Changing

30 August 2026 Bag in box 2 min read

These days you do not need to open a bottle every time you want to drink well.

Bag in box is a practical, sensible system that keeps wine fresh for weeks after opening, with nothing wasted. The wine sits in a sealed pouch and the tap draws from it without letting air back in — which is the thing that does for an open bottle after a day or two.

Quality, simplicity, and always ready when you want it. That is the whole argument. It suits a glass with dinner on a Tuesday far better than a bottle you feel obliged to finish.

We have already chosen this system, to give you the best.

At Divino Urban Cellar you will find a wide selection of wines in bag in box — Sicilian reds, whites and frizzanti among them. Come and have a look; we will be here.
