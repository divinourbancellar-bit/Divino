Sicilian Amaro: Bitter-Sweet Secrets

30 August 2026 Event: Wed 27 May, 18:00 2 min read

Explore Sicily’s bitter-sweet secrets: a tasting session given entirely to Sicilian amaro.

Amaro is the part of an Italian drinks cabinet that visitors most often skip and locals never do. Every one is a different recipe of herbs, roots and citrus peel, and the Sicilian ones lean on what grows here — bitter orange above all.

Tasting four side by side is the quickest way to stop treating “amaro” as one thing. Some are gentle and orange-led; others are properly medicinal. Knowing which you like changes what you order after dinner for the rest of your life.
