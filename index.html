Who we are

A little corner of Provence, in Sicily

Divino is a small enoteca in the heart of Cefalù, where Sicilian wines are poured by the people who chose them. Its roots run out to our land in the countryside, among lavender rows, vines and the olive trees that give Massimo’s own olive oil.
