Carricante Brut Metodo Classico

Planeta · Carricante · Etna

Traditional-method Carricante from 850 m on Etna’s north slope. Citrus, bread crust and a fresh, mineral finish. 24 months on the lees.

From one of Sicily’s leading wine estates, known for bringing out the island’s different terroirs through five separate wineries. Catarratto “Miano”

Castellucci Miano · Catarratto · Madonie

Mountain white from old vines at almost 1,000 m in the Madonie. White flowers, green apple, mineral and lively.

Read Manfredi’s note

Made by a small cooperative in Valledolmo, formed by several families whose mission is to support mountain winegrowing and bring back the old grape varieties of the Madonie. This Catarratto comes from old bush vines and younger trellised vines at almost 1,000 metres, on limestone soil. Wide swings between day and night temperatures and active limestone give a Catarratto of great tension, with aromas of white flowers and green apple, mineral and with lively acidity.

Grillo “della Timpa”

Feudo Montoni · Grillo · Cammarata

Artisan Grillo from the high hills of Cammarata. Orange blossom, citrus and herbs, with a mineral streak.

Read Manfredi’s note

From an artisan estate with roots going back to 1469, run today by winegrower Fabio Sireci, guardian of very old clones of native Sicilian grapes. This Grillo is born on the high hills of Cammarata, between the Madonie and the Agrigento countryside, a cool, breezy area with clay and sand soils, giving an intense, long white with aromas of orange blossom, citrus and herbs, and a lovely mineral streak. Aged in concrete tanks after a few days of skin contact.

Zibibbo Secco “Gagliardetta”

Curatolo Arini · Zibibbo · Gulf of Castellammare

Single-vineyard dry Zibibbo overlooking the Gulf of Castellammare. Citrus and white flowers, with hints of sage and mint.

Read Manfredi’s note

From a historic Marsala winery founded in 1875, whose labels were designed by the architect Ernesto Basile. Gagliardetta is a pure Zibibbo from a single vineyard on an east-facing hill at 350 metres, looking out over the Gulf of Castellammare. Sandy, clay soil for this dry-vinified Zibibbo: very fresh, with a rich, complex bouquet of citrus and white flowers over hints of sage and mint.

Etna Bianco Superiore

Barone di Villagrande · Carricante · Etna

Volcanic white from the slopes of Etna. Mineral, crisp and elegant.

Read Manfredi’s note

From one of Etna’s oldest wine estates, active on the volcano since 1727 and keeper of old vines on the mountain’s eastern slope. This Etna Bianco comes from Milo, the only municipality where the “Superiore” designation is allowed for wines based on Carricante. It grows from volcanic sand and ash on the side of the mountain that faces the sea and is aged for at least ten months in steel. It stands out for its floral and white-fruit aromas, and is harmonious thanks to its freshness and pronounced minerality.

Etna Bianco “Valcerasa”

Alice Bonaccorsi – Valcerasa · Carricante · Etna

A small family estate on Etna. A deeper, more refined volcanic white.

Read Manfredi’s note

From one of the most authentic and interesting artisan estates on Etna, a family winery led by Alice Bonaccorsi, focused on natural practices and respect for the vine. This Carricante comes from Contrada Croce Monaci, on the northern slope of Etna at about 750 metres. The volcanic soil, particularly rich in stones, supports bush-trained vines and gives a pleasantly mineral white that develops precious petrol notes over time. A small share of the wine is macerated on its skins before ageing for two years in steel.

Etna Rosato “Alnus”

I Custodi delle Vigne dell’Etna · Nerello Mascalese · Etna

A pale, delicate rosé from Etna. Red berries, freshness and a salty finish.

Read Manfredi’s note

From an estate founded with the mission of safeguarding Etna’s old bush-trained vineyards and its terraced landscape, with the help of local craftsmen: the Vigneri. This rosé comes from the northern slope of the volcano, from high-altitude bush-trained vineyards on lava-stone terraces. A blend of Nerello Mascalese and Cappuccio, it shows a pale pink colour, with aromas of pomegranate and wild strawberry over a mineral, smoky background.

Perricone

Ferreri · Perricone · Western Sicily

The same rare grape from a family winery in western Sicily. Rich and rustic.

Read Manfredi’s note

From a very small winery in Santa Ninfa, founded in 2003 in the province of Trapani, to celebrate the history, culture and produce of the land. This pure Perricone comes from Contrada Calviano, on mid-height hills with a soil locally called Baiata, completely white and strongly calcareous. Frappato “Nerocapitano”

Lamoresca · Frappato · South-east Sicily

A light, juicy red full of fresh cherries. Delicious slightly chilled.

Read Manfredi’s note

From an artisan estate led by Filippo Rizzo, farmed on natural principles with biodynamic techniques and no invasive intervention in the cellar. This Frappato comes from San Michele di Ganzeria, hilly inland country with limestone and sandy soils, which give a light, lively, juicy red with notes of fresh cherry, pomegranate and sweet spices. On the palate it is very easy to drink, and fresh.

Etna Rosso “Valcerasa”

Alice Bonaccorsi – Valcerasa · Nerello Mascalese · Etna

Elegant volcanic red from Etna. Red fruit, fine tannins, a mineral finish.

Read Manfredi’s note

From one of the most authentic and interesting artisan estates on Etna, a family winery led by Alice Bonaccorsi, focused on natural practices and respect for the vine. This Etna Rosso is made from Nerello Mascalese with 20% Nerello Cappuccio, grown as bush vines in the municipality of Randazzo, on the northern slope of the volcano at 750 metres. After a long ageing in steel and in bottle, it becomes a broad, complex red with floral pot-pourri, moderate minerality and a spicy background.

Nero d’Avola “Vrucara”

Feudo Montoni · Nero d’Avola · Sicily

Our special bottle. Made from very old vines, deep and complex, for a great occasion.

Read Manfredi’s note

From an artisan estate with roots going back to 1469, run today by winegrower Fabio Sireci, guardian of very old clones of native Sicilian grapes. This Nero d’Avola is a selection from the estate’s oldest vines, with ungrafted vines up to 150 years old, grown as bush vines at high altitude on sandy, clay soil.
