Delivery

Same-day delivery, free within 7 km

Delivery & pickup Order by WhatsApp

Staying in Cefalù? We bring Sicilian wine to your villa, holiday rental or apartment — free within 7 km of the cellar, and the same day if you order by 12:00.

How to order

Message us on WhatsApp at +39 331 253 0729, tell us where you are staying and what you like, and we will put together a mixed case for you. Not sure whether you are within 7 km? Just ask and we will confirm.

Choose your mixed case

A mixed case of 6 bottles is right for a short stay or a special dinner. A mixed case of 12 suits longer stays, or sharing with friends and family.

How it reaches you

Villa and holiday rental delivery — we bring the wine directly to you. Apartment delivery — convenient and fast. Same-day delivery — order by 12:00 and receive it the same day. Or pick up at Divino Urban Cellar and visit us in the heart of Cefalù.
