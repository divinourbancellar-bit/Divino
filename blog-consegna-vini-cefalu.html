Sicilian Wine, Delivered to Your Door

30 August 2026 Delivery & pickup 2 min read

Staying in Cefalù? Let Divino Urban Cellar help you discover the best wines Sicily has to offer — without carrying them up the hill yourself.

We carefully select authentic wines from local wineries and small producers for an unforgettable experience. You choose a mixed case and we bring it to you.

Choose your mixed case

A mixed case of 6 bottles is right for a short stay or a special dinner. A mixed case of 12 suits longer stays, or sharing with friends and family.

How it reaches you

Villa and holiday rental delivery — we bring the wine directly to you. Apartment delivery — convenient and fast. Same-day delivery — order by 12:00 and receive it the same day. Or pick up at Divino Urban Cellar and visit us in the heart of Cefalù.

Local wines, authentic Sicily, memorable moments.
